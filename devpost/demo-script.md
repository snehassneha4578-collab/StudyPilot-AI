# StudyPilot AI Demo Cue Sheet

Target length: about 2 minutes 15 seconds (within the required 1-3 minute range). This is an action guide with short factual talking points, not narration to read verbatim.

## Before recording: hands-on verification checklist

Complete these checks in a dedicated browser profile before recording. Do not overwrite a personal saved plan.

- [ ] Open the current Overview and confirm it shows “Plan your studies with confidence,” the “Create My Study Plan” button, and the three quick-start topics: “Subjects & topics,” “Exam dates,” and “Your daily time.” The current first-visit page does not show progress metrics or an illustrative timetable.
- [ ] Enter a feasible plan with at least two subjects and multiple topics, set future exam dates, and choose enough daily study time. Use values you enter in the form; do not present artwork or invented figures as live app data.
- [ ] Generate the plan and confirm the My Study Plan timetable shows the subjects and topics you entered, with estimated durations and revision sessions.
- [ ] Complete one planned session. Confirm it changes to Complete and that Progress & Insights updates from the saved plan.
- [ ] Choose a different, still-incomplete planned session. Confirm a suggested slot is available; reschedule it and verify the completed session remains completed and in its original slot.
- [ ] Refresh the browser and verify the saved plan, completion, and reschedule persist. This check has not yet been independently verified for the recording. If it does not pass, omit the refresh demonstration and make no persistence claim.
- [ ] Check the exact screens, text, dates, and suggested slot you plan to show. Avoid fixed example dates; show only dates offered by the current plan.

Any unchecked behavior remains a manual verification item. Do not imply that a behavior worked if you have not observed it in the browser.
